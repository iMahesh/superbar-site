Privacy Statement

Effective November 6, 2026

This statement explains what happens to your information when you visit superbar.cc and when you email us. Questions are welcome at hello@superbar.cc.

Your choice comes first

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept cookies and similar tracking tools. Until you choose Accept, Microsoft Clarity doesn’t load, and Google’s tags run in a mode that sets no cookies. If you choose Decline, that stays the case. You can change your choice at any time:

Microsoft Clarity

If you accept, we partner with Microsoft Clarity to see how you use and interact with our website, through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay. This shows us where people click, how far they scroll, and where a page is confusing, so we can make the site better. Clarity captures website usage data with first- and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies. We and Microsoft collect and use this data. We don’t use it for advertising, but Microsoft may use it as its own privacy statement describes.

For more about how Microsoft collects and uses your data, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Google Tag Manager

We use Google Tag Manager to manage measurement tags on this site. Until you accept, Google’s tags run in consent mode with storage denied, so they don’t set analytics or advertising cookies. If you accept, they may set cookies to count visits and see which pages are popular. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting

superbar.cc is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, such as your IP address, to run and secure the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Email

If you email hello@superbar.cc, for example to hear when the ready-to-install app ships, we use your address and message to reply and to tell you when it’s available. We don’t sell it or share it for marketing. Ask us at any time and we’ll delete it.

The Superbar app

This statement covers the website. The Superbar app keeps its settings on your Mac and doesn’t send them to us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to how it’s used. Email hello@superbar.cc and we’ll help. For data held by Microsoft or Google, you can also use the links above.

Changes

If this statement changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
